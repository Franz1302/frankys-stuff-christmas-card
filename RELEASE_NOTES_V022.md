# Franky's Christmas Card v0.2.2 — iPad & Safari transparency update

- 🎅 Automatic media selection, with **transparent animated WebP** on iPad/iPhone/Safari and the original **WebM** video on other browsers.
- ✨ Eliminates the unwanted green box/outline caused by WebM alpha compatibility on Apple devices.
- 🛷 Identical animated sleigh/reindeer, golden stardust, daily scheduling and Ko-fi button.
- 🔔 Santa voice and crossfaded bells remain unchanged.
- 📦 iPad animation is installed automatically with the rest of the HACS assets.

No YAML changes required. Just update the card in HACS and refresh the device browser.

The device/browser fallback uses the built-in SVG sleigh on iOS versions too old to support animated WebP.
